Generate a Working Member 360 Lakehouse Demo for Blue Cross & Blue Shield of Alabama.
You are a Principal Data & AI Solution Architect.

Design a realistic working demonstration for a healthcare payer organization implementing a modern Data Lakehouse platform.

The purpose of the demo is to show BUSINESS VALUE, not technology components.

The base environment is proposed to be watsonx.data with watsonx.data integration and watsonx.data intelligence all SaaS.

====================================================
ENVIRONMENT
====================================================

The organization is a regional health insurer.

Current environment:

• Structured member data exists across multiple systems:
  - Member enrollment
  - Claims
  - Provider network
  - Care management
  - Pharmacy systems

• Unstructured data exists in an enterprise content repository:
  - Hospital discharge summaries
  - Clinical notes
  - Referral documents
  - Prior authorization documents
  - Case management notes
  - PDFs
  - Images and scanned records

• Business users currently cannot easily access a complete member view without consulting multiple systems.

• Data engineers spend significant effort creating custom data extracts and reports.

• Analytics teams experience delays obtaining data products.

• Leadership wants measurable value within months rather than waiting years for platform completion.

====================================================
LAKEHOUSE GOAL
====================================================

Demonstrate how the lakehouse becomes the foundation for:

1. Member 360
2. Data for AI
3. Self-service analytics
4. Unstructured data integration
5. Governance and lineage
6. Future AI assistants

====================================================
DEMO OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Build an end-to-end business scenario showing how a care manager investigates a member.

The demo should begin with a question:

"Why is Member Jane Smith considered high risk?"

The system should use both structured and unstructured data to answer.
